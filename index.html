Emmanuel Oluwajomiloju Adewale

Founder & CEO — Meisophant Digital Services

profile

Emmanuel Oluwajomiloju Adewale — known in the community as CEO AEO — is a 13-year-old developer and entrepreneur based in Lagos, Nigeria. He founded and runs Meisophant Digital Services, a small studio building custom web platforms, cloud infrastructure, and business applications for clients.

He started young: Meisophant began in 2021 as a game-development idea he had at age nine. A year later he redirected the project toward something more practical — solving real logistics and scaling problems for businesses — and spent the next three years teaching himself HTML, CSS, and JavaScript from scratch, rather than relying on drag-and-drop site builders.
